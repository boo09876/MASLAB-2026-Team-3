About the Project

DSOX is our MASLAB competition robot. Our goal is to build a fully autonomous system capable of perception, planning, and control in a dynamic environment. This page serves as our internal and external logbook.

Progress Timeline

  • Electrical Setup — Soldering, wiring motors and servos, power buttons, Raven board integration, and initial power distribution Done
  • Compute Setup — Raspberry Pi setup, OS configuration, device communication, and deployment workflow Done
  • Motor & Servo Control — DC motor drivers, servo control, and low-level actuation interfaces Done
  • Sensing & State Estimation — IMU setup, sensor integration, and basic state estimation Done
  • Control — PID tuning and motion profiling for stable, repeatable movement In Progress
  • Vision — Camera setup, image processing pipeline, calibration, and homography In Progress
  • Autonomy — Full integration of vision, control, and decision-making for autonomous operation To Do
  • Competition-Ready Autonomy — Reliable end-to-end autonomous operation, scoring, and recovery under competition conditions To Do

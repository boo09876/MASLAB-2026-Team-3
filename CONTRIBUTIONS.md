# Contribution Notes

This repository was developed collaboratively by members of MIT MASLAB 2026 Team 3.

## Hendery Chen — Head Programmer

My primary work centered on robot motion and autonomous navigation. Major areas included:

- PID-based movement and heading-control logic
- Encoder/IMU odometry and pose updates
- Motion profiling for acceleration/deceleration
- Pure-pursuit-style path-following experiments and Bézier path generation
- Integration and debugging of movement behavior on the physical robot
- Smaller contributions to perception integration/testing

I contributed substantially to the shared codebase, but I do not claim authorship of all files or all systems in this repository.

## Team Code

Computer-vision, localization, state-machine, and other subsystems were developed collaboratively. Some files represent experiments or alternate implementations created by other team members and are preserved here to reflect the original competition development process.
